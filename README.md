# Candy House Adventure - playable build

Play: https://larinastudy01-lab.github.io/unfinished-tales-play/

Version 0.10.0 makes the first floor an untimed platform exploration. Push the living-room box so your companion opens drawer stairs. Land on the kitchen plate so your companion fixes a tray. Open the storage footbridge so your companion raises the loading lift. Each physically built route leads to its high puzzle station; supports stay in place and missed jumps return to the last safe landing without losing progress.

The player solves both living-room merge boards, the kitchen recipe and two wrapped-product deliveries, and the storage side-slot plus red-bar exit. Companion mechanisms are necessary for the next step. Personally collect gifts, use the new shortcuts, fill all three hallway badge slots, collect the key and unlock the original ending. Choose Hansel or Gretel once; the other sibling supports automatically.

Keyboard: A/D or arrows move, Space/W/Up jumps (tap low, hold high), E interacts, Q returns from a room. Puzzle controls are shown before play. Z undoes a puzzle move; R resets the current board while keeping earned permanent progress. Standard mapped PS-style controller: D-pad/left stick moves, cross jumps or interacts by context, circle cancels, L1 undoes, R1 resets, Options pauses. Touch uses the displayed icon controls and puzzle gestures. The third-floor runner and second-floor knowledge chase retain their controls, including Q/square gummy boost and keyboard 1/2/3 knowledge answers.
