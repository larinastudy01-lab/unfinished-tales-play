# Candy House Adventure - playable build

Play: https://larinastudy01-lab.github.io/unfinished-tales-play/

Version 0.9.0 updates the second-floor witch chase. Each of the eight original knowledge questions fills the game stage with three readable corridor entrances. The chase freezes through reading, the three-second answer clock and the selected-door zoom. Your choice joins a real lane. Correct answers keep the original speed reward and bring your companion forward with a biscuit shield. Wrong answers and timeouts enter unshielded lanes where a warned ground wave can be jumped or avoided on a low platform. Three answer mistakes still restart the chase.

At the fourth correct answer, the companion blocks the spell and waits behind a cookie gate. Land on the side plate to lift it and help your companion through. Missing the plate leaves a visible elevated detour and a later reunion. Both selected siblings use the same controls; no character switching or second controller is needed. The third-floor runner, first-floor rooms, badge/key flow and ending remain playable.

Keyboard: A/D or arrows move; Space/W/Up jump (tap low, hold high); Q uses a collected gummy. Knowledge questions: 1, 2 or 3 answers directly. Standard mapped PS-style gamepad: D-pad chooses answers, cross confirms; running uses D-pad/left stick, cross jumps, square uses a gummy, Options pauses. Touch uses the displayed controls and taps the chosen answer entrance. Confirm/jump must release after a question before the next jump is accepted. Full instructions appear before play.
